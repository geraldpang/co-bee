What is this tab?
Reconciliation just means checking that money in matches money out. Every time a buyer pays through the HitPay checkout, HitPay takes a small processing fee, then pays the rest to your bank account in a batch ("payout") every few days. This tab lines up each buyer payment against those payouts, so you can see exactly what was collected, what HitPay kept as fees, and what actually landed in your bank — useful for your own records or for handing to an accountant/bookkeeper.
