Our origin
Born from a real problem

Co-bee was born during the COVID-19 pandemic, when we saw how hard it was for community hosts to run group-buy with limited tools.

As orders grew, hosts were juggling payments, deliveries, and product lists — often across chats and spreadsheets. It was stressful, time-consuming, and far from ideal.

So we built Co-bee. What started as a simple solution to help friends quickly became a platform that brings people together, makes group-buy effortless, and helps communities grow stronger — together.
